# Art Direction — Grok Bots Kart Racing

This document establishes **principles only**. The Art Director / Coordinator
fills the TBD sections after the playable baseline exists. Nothing here locks
detailed art direction prematurely.

## Non-negotiables

- **Original Grok Bots IP** — every character, kart, track, and asset is
  original. No imitation of Nintendo (or any third-party) characters, tracks,
  karts, items, UI, music, or artwork.
- **Expressive geometry** — characters are built from bold shapes and forms,
  not surface detail.
- **Shape-driven personalities** — a bot's silhouette communicates who it is
  before it moves.
- **Strong silhouettes** — readable at a glance, in motion, at speed.
- **Readable color hierarchy** — primary/secondary/accent discipline per
  character, kart, and track region.
- **Recognizable at race-camera distance** — characters and rivals must read
  clearly from the standard chase view.
- **Stylized, not photorealistic** — cohesive stylization over realism.
- **Tactile materials** — surfaces feel touchable: soft vinyl, painted metal,
  rubber, glowing accents.
- **Joyful presentation** — squash, bounce, sparkle; the world wants to be played in.
- **Strong secondary motion** — antennae, suspension, flags, particles carry life.
- **Premium first-party polish target** — Mario Kart et al. are *quality
  benchmarks only*: responsiveness, readability, delight, production value.
- **Distinctive original karts** — each kart is a character extension of its driver.
- **Memorable track landmarks** — every track has navigable, ownable set pieces.
- **Consistent visual universe** — characters, karts, tracks, items, and UI
  feel like one world.
- **UI related to the 3D world** — interface styling shares the world's
  shapes, colors, and materials.
- **Clarity at high speed** — nothing important is ever ambiguous mid-race.

## Established direction (2026-09-13, Coordinator as Art Director)

Canonical concept seeds generated — see
`assets/media/concepts/characters/grokbot-explore-{A,B,C}.png`.

- **Roster system — shape archetype = personality:** each racer owns one
  geometric vocabulary. Approved seeds:
  - **A "Rounded"** (canonical signature bot): sphere/capsule vocabulary,
    dome-visor face, candy-stripe antenna, cream/coral/teal. Friendly.
  - **B "Chunky"** (heavy archetype): box vocabulary, CRT pixel-face,
    mitten limbs, sunflower/navy. Power.
  - **C "Wedge"** (speed archetype): triangle/blade vocabulary, fin head,
    angular armor, violet/cyan. Aggressive.
- **Face language:** dark visor/screen + glowing geometric features
  (eyes, mouth) — readable at distance, animatable, emissive-friendly.
- **Kart rule:** the kart echoes its driver's shape vocabulary and palette
  (A's dome windshield = dome head; B's squared fenders = cube body;
  C's blade fins = wedge limbs). Kart is a character extension.
- **Palette philosophy:** light/neutral base + one bold accent color +
  glowing accent lights. High value contrast between face visor and body.
- **Material language:** soft vinyl / painted metal bodies, rubber wheels,
  emissive accent strips — tactile, toy-like, matte-with-glow not glossy.
- **Lighting language:** bright cheerful key light + sky fill; soft shadows;
  slight saturation lift. Think "premium toy commercial."
- **Scale:** per ADR-002 — kart 1.6×2.6 m, racer ~1.2–1.6 m, wheel 0.45 m.
